How Tonight works

Tonight compares genres, themes, cast and directors with your ratings using simple weighted scores. No AI is used. With only a few ratings, the suggestions are a starting point.

Romantic comedy includes films labelled with both Romance and Comedy, explicitly tagged as a romcom, or confirmed by Netflix’s own genre descriptions. We search all three routes. Missing catalogue labels can still leave films out. Action & adventure includes either genre.

Favour AD changes the order, never the eligibility of a film. Confirmed English AD comes first, followed by likely, possible and unknown; your taste orders each group. Confirmed evidence matches the UK service. Likely Prime evidence lists English AD but has no UK account verification. Possible evidence comes from another service or version. Evidence expires after 30 days. The checked index is incomplete; unknown does not mean no AD.

Advert-supported subscription tiers may have a different catalogue. This first version uses base Netflix and Prime availability.

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

About Tonight

Find a film included with your UK Netflix, Prime Video, Apple TV or Disney+ subscription. Rentals, purchases and extra paid channels are left out.

Rate a few favourites and dislikes to help Tonight learn your taste. It compares genres, themes, cast and directors with your ratings — no AI needed. Films you’ve watched leave the recommendations, but you can still look them up with Search.

Favour AD keeps your options open. It puts confirmed English audio description first, followed by the provider’s own AD badge, weaker evidence and then unknown. Within each group, your taste still decides the order. Turn it off to sort by taste alone.

Netflix often comes first because we have stronger AD evidence for it. Apple lists AD languages; Disney’s own AD badge gets high priority even when it doesn’t identify the language. Both UK and US English AD count equally. Prime’s information is less certain. Unknown doesn’t mean a film has no AD, so we favour it rather than filter films out.

Choose a genre or search for a particular film. More films adds to the list. Availability can change, and both genre labels and AD information can have gaps.
